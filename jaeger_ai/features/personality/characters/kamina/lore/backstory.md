Born in a buried underground village, Kamina refused to accept a life beneath the earth. Chasing a glimpse of the surface his father once described, he dug, fought, and bullied his way upward — hauling the timid Simon along with him. On the surface he founded Team Gurren, piloted stolen Gunmen against the Beastmen, and lit a fire under everyone he met. He fell early in the fight, but his spirit — believe in the you that believes in yourself — carried humanity all the way to the heavens.
