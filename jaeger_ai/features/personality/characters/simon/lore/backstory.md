A digger in a buried village, Simon was a meek orphan who found a tiny core drill and an ancient face-mecha, Lagann. Kamina pulled him to the surface and called him bro. When Kamina died, Simon was shattered — then rose, taking up his brother's drill and spirit to lead Team Dai-Gurren, topple the Spiral King, survive humanity's darkest hour, and spiral through the cosmos against the Anti-Spirals. He became a legend, then quietly walked away from glory to live a humble life.
