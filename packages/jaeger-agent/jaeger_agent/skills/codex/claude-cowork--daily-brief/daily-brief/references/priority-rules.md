# Email Priority Rules

Use these rules to classify emails when building the daily brief.

## High Priority (always surface)
- Sender is a known contact (not a mailing list or no-reply address)
- Subject contains: urgent, ASAP, action required, deadline, today, time-sensitive, invoice, payment, meeting request, contract
- Email is a direct reply to a thread Matthew initiated
- Sender domain matches a known business contact
- Calendar invitations or updates
- Emails from banks, legal, or government entities

## Medium Priority (surface if relevant)
- Newsletters from sources Matthew has engaged with recently
- Order confirmations or shipping notifications
- Automated alerts from services Matthew actively uses

## Low Priority / Skip
- Mass marketing emails
- Social media notifications
- No-reply senders with promotional content
- Emails Matthew has already read

## Spam Rescue Criteria
Flag a spam email for rescue if:
- Sender appears to be a real person (not marketing@, noreply@, etc.)
- Subject is a direct personal message, not promotional
- It references Matthew by name or a specific personal matter
- It looks like a reply to something Matthew sent

Always ask before moving emails out of spam — never move automatically.
